gpu-sankhya

Parses Indian informal number shorthand in Hinglish and Devanagari (sava lakh, dedh crore, डेढ़ लाख, सवा करोड़, 2.5L, 20k, 2-3 lakh) into numbers, entirely in your browser — an ~18k-parameter char CNN, 27 KB gzipped.
