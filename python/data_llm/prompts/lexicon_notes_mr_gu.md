### Units / prefixes
- शंभर / से (100) — "से" seen as a colloquial suffix on tens (e.g. दोनशे), "शंभर" as standalone.
- हजार (1,000)
- लाख (100,000)
- कोटी (10,000,000)
- सव्वा × 1.25 (सव्वा लाख = 125000)
- दीड × 1.5 (दीड लाख = 150000)
- अडीच × 2.5 (अडीच लाख = 250000)
- साडे + numword (+0.5 of the next unit; साडेतीन लाख = 350000, साडेपाच हजार = 5500). Written both as one word (साडेतीन) and observed alt spelling साडे तीन (space).
- पावणे + numword (−0.25 short of the next unit; पावणे दोन लाख = 175000, पावणे तीन लाख = 275000). Also seen contracted पावणेदोन (no space).
- अर्धा (0.5x a unit; अर्धा लाख = 50000, अर्धा कोटी = 5000000)
- पाव (0.25x a unit; पाव लाख = 25000, पाव कोटी = 2500000)

### Currency markers
रुपये, रु., रु, रुपया, ₹ (prefix form ₹500, or spelled-out "रु. 1200")

### Plural / indefinite (money-ish, no exact number — negatives)
लाखो लोक, लाखो रुपयांचा (तोटा), हजारो लोक, करोडोंची (उलाढाल), लखपती, करोडपती

### Alternative spellings observed while writing
- साडेतीन vs साडे तीन (joined/split)
- पावणे दोन vs पावणेदोन (joined/split)
- रु. vs रु (with/without period)
- तेहेतीस (33) alt: तेहतीस
- शहात्तर (76) / शहाण्णव (96) — “श” cluster spellings vary regionally (some write सहात्तर / सहाण्णव)
- ऐंशी (80) sometimes typed अंशी on phones (typo drop of nasal ं rendering)

## Notes for the lexicon pack builder
- Both languages: digits + Latin unit letters (k, L, cr) are used exactly as in Hindi (2.5L, 20k, 1.5cr).
- Both languages: grouped digit strings follow Indian lakh/crore comma grouping (e.g. ૨૭,૪૮,૯૪૫ / २७,४८,९४५).
- Latin loanwords mixed in freely: budget, flat, CTC, EMI, rent, salary, deal, final, loan, offer, package, bonus, stock, invest, LPA, cr, k, L.
- Negatives without any number: money-ish plural/indefinite words (लाखो लोक / લાખો લોકો, करोडपती / કરોડપતિ) must NOT be tagged as quantities.
